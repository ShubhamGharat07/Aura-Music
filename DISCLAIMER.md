## Privacy and permissions

- Aura Music does not require an account and does not include an analytics or
  crash-reporting SDK.
- Search, catalog, artwork, stream, and lyrics requests are sent to third-party
  providers so the app can function. Those providers receive the request data
  needed to serve the content, subject to their own policies.
- Library data, history, playlists, likes, and downloads are stored locally.
  The local database is encrypted at rest, and its key is kept in the platform
  secure store.
- On Android, cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled, app lock
  is available, and screenshot blocking can be enabled. The iOS runner is
  present but has not been fully production-configured.
- Permissions are requested only for features that use them: network access,
  media playback, notifications, biometrics for app lock, and Bluetooth for the
  optional experimental party mode.
