## Privacy and permissions

- **On your device.** Aura Music requires no account and includes no analytics or crash-reporting SDK. Likes, playlists, and listening history are stored in an encrypted local database. The encryption key is kept in platform secure storage, and offline audio is saved in app-private storage. Clear local data under **Settings → Privacy & security → Clear all data**.
- **Network requests.** Search, playback, artwork, lyrics, and update checks contact external services. Those services can receive your IP address and request data such as search terms or song and artist details. Their own privacy policies govern their handling of those requests.
- **Device access.** Android permissions support playback and notifications, optional biometric app lock, optional nearby Bluetooth sessions, and APK installation for in-app updates. On older Android versions, nearby Bluetooth discovery can require location permission.

Keep personal information and confidential materials out of the report. Rights holders can use GitHub’s copyright reporting process for material hosted on GitHub.
